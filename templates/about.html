Who we are

We are a community of people interested in the mathematics of autonomous systems, robotics, machine learning, control and related fields. We draw attention to these topics and bring people into academic initiatives around them.

No experience required. If the field interests you — you want to understand it, to talk with people who share your interests, or in time to get involved in research — join us ↗
